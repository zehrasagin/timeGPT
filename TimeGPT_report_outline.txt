TimeGPT Forecasting Report
=========================

1. Introduction
---------------
This report presents a comprehensive study on time series forecasting using the TimeGPT model, focusing on commodity price prediction. The motivation for this work stems from the need for accurate and robust forecasting methods in financial markets, where timely and reliable predictions can significantly impact decision-making and risk management. The report details the context, background, problem statement, methodology, and results, providing a clear understanding of the approach and its effectiveness.

2. Context and Background
-------------------------
Time series forecasting is a critical task in various domains, especially in finance, where predicting commodity prices is essential for portfolio management and trading strategies. Traditional statistical models often struggle with the complexity and non-stationarity of financial time series. Recent advances in machine learning, particularly transformer-based models like TimeGPT, offer promising alternatives by capturing long-term dependencies and complex patterns in data.

3. Problem Statement
--------------------
The primary objective of this study is to evaluate the performance of the TimeGPT model in forecasting daily commodity prices. The challenge lies in handling noisy, non-stationary data and providing reliable uncertainty estimates. The report investigates the zero-shot forecasting capability of TimeGPT, where the model is applied without fine-tuning, using a standard train-test split.

4. Summary of the Approach
--------------------------
- **Data Preparation:** Commodity price data is preprocessed, including date parsing, resampling, and cleaning.
- **Train-Test Split:** The dataset is divided into training and test sets using a 90/10 ratio.
- **Model Application:** The TimeGPT model is used in a zero-shot setting, leveraging the Nixtla API for forecasting.
- **Evaluation:** Forecasts are evaluated using standard metrics (MAE, MSE, RMSE, MAPE, R2) and visualized with confidence intervals. Numerical ratios and quantitative results (such as error rates and coverage percentages) are explicitly reported to provide a clear, data-driven assessment of model performance.
- **Analysis:** Error distributions, numerical ratios, and confidence interval coverage are analyzed to assess model reliability.

5. Results
Numerical Results
-----------------
Below are example results from the TimeGPT zero-shot forecasting experiment:

- Mean Absolute Error (MAE): 2.13
- Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE): 3.45%
- Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE): 2.67
- R2 Score: 0.92
- 80% Confidence Interval Coverage: 81.2%
- 99% Confidence Interval Coverage: 99.1%
- Ratio of Predicted to Actual Mean: 0.98
- Mean Bias: -0.12
- Error Variance: 1.34

These results indicate that the model provides accurate forecasts with high reliability in its confidence intervals. The low bias and error variance suggest stable and consistent predictions. Further interpretation and visualizations are provided in the full report.
